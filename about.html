General Services

What is the Miyawaki Method, and why is it special? +
The Miyawaki Method is an advanced Japanese technique for creating ultra-dense native forests on degraded land. Trees are planted densely (3–5 plants per square meter), prioritized as local native species, and managed to grow 10 times faster than conventional plantations, reaching maturity in 15–20 years with minimal intervention after the first 2–3 years.
How does EarthwormX choose which species to plant? +
Every site is surveyed, and only species native to the local region are selected. This supports biodiversity, ecosystem resilience, and ensures long-term survival of the forest. Each project is independently designed with a custom mix of canopy, understory, shrub, and groundcover species.
How do you restore barren or degraded land? +
Our process starts with deep soil analysis, organic matter enrichment, and regenerative preparation using compost, biochar, and other natural amendments. Next, we implement dense, diverse planting using the Miyawaki or food forest model, rapidly transforming dead land into thriving green zones within a few years.
What's included in your farm layout and landscape master plans? +
EarthwormX provides complete eco-friendly designs—landscape analysis, efficient farm layouts, water management, biodiversity zones, and plant selection, all customized for productivity and conservation. Our plans balance agriculture, ecosystem health, and long-term resilience.
What maintenance does a Miyawaki or food forest need? +
After planting, we monitor, water, and weed intensively for about two to three years. Once established, the forest becomes self-sustaining, requiring minimal human input beyond basic upkeep and seasonal care.
How do I know my plantation or project is making an impact? +
You receive regular progress reports with photos, plant survival rates, and CO₂ offset estimates. Each project site's transformation is documented and tracked, so you can see your legacy grow. Many of our clients also visit their forest sites for milestone events or ecological monitoring.
Does EarthwormX work with individuals, businesses, or landowners? +
We partner with all—eco-conscious families, farmland owners, CSR teams, and business investors. Anyone motivated to restore land and create ecological wealth can start a project with us.
How soon do you see results after plantation? +
Miyawaki forests and well-designed food forests rapidly establish themselves. Within just one to three years, you'll see robust native vegetation, increased biodiversity, and microclimates that support birds, insects, and soil life.
What are the benefits of bamboo plantation and propagation? +
Bamboo restores soil structure, sequesters carbon, prevents erosion, and creates a sustainable resource for communities and landowners—all with minimal ongoing maintenance after establishment.
Can I visit my project site or participate in planting? +
Absolutely! EarthwormX welcomes partners and supporters for site visits, awareness events, and hands-on planting days. Community involvement is encouraged to foster a lifelong connection with your restored landscape.

Irrigation Systems

What types of irrigation systems do you install? +
We specialize in drip irrigation, mist and fog systems, pop-up sprinklers, and drip line setups, customized for each farm's unique needs and crops.
How does drip irrigation differ from sprinkler irrigation? +
Drip irrigation delivers water slowly and directly to the roots, maximizing efficiency and reducing evaporation. Sprinklers spray water over the plants and soil, suitable for large areas but with higher evaporation.
What is the benefit of mist and fog irrigation? +
Mist and fog systems are ideal for delicate seedlings and plants needing high humidity, providing gentle, even water distribution with minimal waste.
How do you ensure my irrigation system uses water efficiently? +
Our systems use precision delivery, moisture sensors, and smart timers to water only when needed, drastically reducing water waste compared to traditional systems.
How long does it take to install an irrigation system? +
Installation timelines vary by project size but typically range from a few days to weeks, including design, material sourcing, and setup.

Can the system be automated? +
Absolutely. We integrate automation with sensors and timers for hands-free, optimized watering schedules.
Are your plumbing materials customizable? +
Yes, we source materials globally to meet client-specific durability, budget, and project requirements.
Is your irrigation system suitable for all crops? +
We tailor our systems for a wide range of crops, from orchards and vineyards to food forests and vegetable farms.
