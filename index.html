Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process and services.

What areas do you serve?+
We primarily serve the city and surrounding regions. For larger projects, we travel further—tell us about your site and we’ll confirm.
Do you use native plants?+
Yes. We prioritize native, climate-appropriate species for resilience, biodiversity, and low maintenance.
How long does a project take?+
Small installations can be completed in a few days. Full landscape transformations typically take 2–8 weeks depending on scope and season.
Do you provide maintenance?+
We offer ongoing care plans and seasonal tune-ups to keep your landscape thriving.
