Our Services

Natural Land Regeneration & Transformation

We work with landowners and real-estate developers to restore degraded land and unlock its long-term ecological and economic potential.

Our approach includes:

  • • Soil regeneration and land rehabilitation
  • • Ecological landscape planning
  • • Preparing land for sustainable, high-value development.

Miyawaki Forest Creation

We create dense native forests using the Miyawaki method — an advanced ecological approach where diverse native species are planted in high density to accelerate natural forest growth. This method rapidly restores biodiversity, improves local microclimates, and reduces heat stress while transforming land into resilient ecosystems.

Natural & Sustainable Landscape Design

We provide landscape design and implementation services that focus on natural, eco-friendly, and sustainable methods.

Plant Supply

We supply diverse plant varieties for landscaping, farming, and ecological restoration.

• Bamboo • Ornamental Plants • Fruit Plants • Forest Plants • Shade Trees • Medicinal Plants

Intelligent Irrigation Systems

We design intelligent irrigation systems that deliver water efficiently to plant roots while minimizing wastage and supporting healthy plant growth.

  • * Mist Irrigation
  • * Artificial Rain Systems
  • * Drip Irrigation
  • * Pop-up Sprinklers
  • * Rain water harvesting
  • * Creating water bodies

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process and services.

What areas do you serve?+
We primarily serve the city and surrounding regions. For larger projects, we travel further—tell us about your site and we’ll confirm.
Do you use native plants?+
Yes. We prioritize native, climate-appropriate species for resilience, biodiversity, and low maintenance.
How long does a project take?+
Small installations can be completed in a few days. Full landscape transformations typically take 2–8 weeks depending on scope and season.
Do you provide maintenance?+
We offer ongoing care plans and seasonal tune-ups to keep your landscape thriving.
